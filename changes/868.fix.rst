Fixed the ``zhmc partitionlink attach-partition`` and ``detach-partition``
commands, which called methods that do not exist in the zhmcclient package.
They now use ``Partition.attach_network_link()`` / ``detach_network_link()`` for
Hipersockets and SMC-D partition links and ``Partition.attach_ctc_link()`` /
``detach_ctc_link()`` for CTC partition links.
